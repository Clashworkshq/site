Questions

Short answers.

Is the free video really free?

Yes. You fill out a 2-minute form, and I take two a week. I spend 10 minutes on your website, Google listing and how you run things, and record what I'd fix first. You hear back within 7 days either way. No pitch, and you keep it.

Do I need to learn anything technical?

No. I set everything up and show you how it works in plain words. You approve the wording of any message before it goes out to a customer.

Do you need my passwords?

Never. You add me as a user on your own accounts, with the least access the job needs, and you remove me when I'm done. Your accounts stay yours.

What does “your money back” mean, exactly?

Before I start, we agree on one number, like quote requests a week or calls answered, and I set up a counter for it. You pay half once we've written down your starting number and half when it's done. If I can't show you that number 30 days after I finish, I refund my fees within 15 days. It's not a promise the number goes up, and it doesn't apply if the counter gets removed. Full conditions.

Will this get me to the top of Google?

I don't promise rankings or a number of new customers, because nobody can honestly promise those. I promise the fix, a counter that shows what it's doing, and an honest number at 30 days.
