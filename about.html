Clayton Trevino. Finance and Real Estate major at SMU's Cox School of Business. Dallas, Texas.

I spent hundreds of hours learning to make AI do the unglamorous work inside a business: quotes, missed calls, follow-up, reviews, reminders. Now I fix those things for local owners and show the numbers, so you can see whether it worked.

How I got here.

I did not learn to code. I learned to describe a business problem clearly enough that AI could build the fix, then I tested the fix on real businesses until it held up.

It started close to home. My family runs businesses, and watching them up close is where I first saw how much of a good company's week still runs by hand: quotes, follow-up, reminders, the same questions answered over and over. Small, boring problems that quietly cost real time. Those were the first things I learned to fix.

From there I kept going: a booking sync for a vacation rental owner, an internal app for tracking work, a commercial real estate tool that I am still building. Along the way I learned that most local businesses lose money in the same five or six places, and that none of them need a developer to fix.

So that is what Clashworks is. I find the leak, I build the fix, I install tracking so the result is a number and not an opinion, and I hand the template out for free. If I get something wrong, I say so on camera.

How I work.

  • Measured or free. Every fix ships with tracking. If the number cannot be shown at 30 days, you get a refund.
  • Small on purpose. A few engagements at a time, so each one gets built properly and measured honestly.
  • No promises on rankings or lead counts. Nobody can honestly make those. I promise the fix and the measurement.
  • Baseline first. I record where you are before I touch anything, so the before and after are real.
  • Never a shared password. You add me as a user with the lowest access that does the job. Your accounts stay yours.
  • Templates go out free. The files I install for paying clients are the same ones I release with each video.

What I am not: a guru, an agency with a sales team, or a course. I do not manage ad spend. I fix specific things and show you what happened.
