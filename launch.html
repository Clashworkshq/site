From the LAUNCH reel

20 things I check before any site goes live.

The reel covered the seven I see most. Here are all twenty. Each one takes minutes to fix, and every one of them is something a visitor notices.

01

A real 404 page

A broken link should still show your brand and send people home.

02

The main button above the fold

On a phone, the one thing you want people to do is visible without scrolling.

03

Pages that link to each other

Every page points to at least two others, so nobody hits a dead end.

04

A thank-you page after the form

It says what happens next, so people know the message went through.

05

A way back home

Breadcrumbs on a deep site, a clear home link on a small one.

06

Proof, or an honest placeholder

Real case studies with permission, or a plain line saying results go up as they're measured.

07

Five real FAQs

The questions people actually ask before they buy, answered plainly.

08

A response time you can keep

Say how fast you reply, and only promise what you'll really do.

09

A button that stays on screen on phones

The main action stays reachable while people scroll.

10

robots.txt

It lets search engines in and points them to your sitemap.

11

A different title on every page

No two pages share the same title in the browser tab.

12

A description for every page

The line that shows under your link in Google, written for the click.

13

A share image

Send your link to a friend. If the preview is blank, you're missing one.

14

Directions, if people visit you

A map only matters if customers walk through your door.

15

Real reviews only

Real names, with permission. Made-up reviews can get you fined by the FTC.

16

Alt text on images

A short description on photos that matter, so screen readers and Google understand them.

17

Business details search engines can read

Your real name, address and hours in the page's structured data.

18

A privacy page

Needed the moment you have a form, analytics, or an embedded widget.

19

Analytics

Know how many people visit and what they do, with any consent your area requires.

20

A real photo of you

People hire people. A stock handshake does the opposite.
