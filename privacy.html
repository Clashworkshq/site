What I collect

Only what you type into a form. There are two.

  • The Optimization Check application. Your name, your business name, its website and Google listing, an email or phone number to reach you, and your answers about how the business runs. If you ticked items on the homepage picker, those come along too.
  • The Lead Leak Checklist request. One field, your email address.

Nothing is collected from you just for visiting. No account, no sign-in, no hidden fields.

Why I collect it

  • To record your free Optimization Check and send it to you.
  • To send the checklist you asked for.
  • To reply if you asked a question or wanted something built.

I do not sell or rent any of it. I do not add you to a newsletter unless you ask for one. Nothing about your business is published without your written permission.

Where it goes

Form answers are stored by Tally (tally.so), the form tool both forms run on, and then in my own private records for the check. These services have their own privacy policies and are the only outside places your form answers go:

  • Tally receives and stores what you submit and shows the form inside this page.
  • Google Fonts serves the two typefaces on this site, which means your browser requests them from Google.
  • cdnjs (Cloudflare) serves one small animation library on the homepage.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the site itself and sees the usual server logs.

Links to YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, X, LinkedIn, and GitHub take you to those sites, which then apply their own rules.

Cookies and tracking

This site sets no cookies of its own and runs no analytics or advertising trackers. The embedded Tally form may set a functional cookie so the form works. That is the full list. If I add a visitor counter later, this page will say which one and what it records before it goes live.

How long I keep it

Application answers are kept while the check is open and for as long as we might work together, then deleted. Checklist emails are kept only to send the checklist. Anything you ask me to remove is removed, from Tally and from my records.

Your choices

You can ask to see what I hold about you, correct it, or have it deleted, at any time. Reply to any message from me, or send a note through the application form or any Clashworks profile linked in the footer. I answer within seven days.

Terms of use

  • The free Optimization Check is advice, not a promise. It is my honest read of your website, Google listing, and how you run things. What you do with it is your call.
  • No guarantees on rankings or lead counts. Nobody can honestly make those. Paid work comes with a fixed price and a number we agree on up front; if that number cannot be shown at 30 days, you get a refund. The details live in the written agreement we both sign before any paid work starts.
  • The free kits are yours to use. They are released under the MIT license on GitHub, with no warranty. Test them before you rely on them.
  • The site content is mine. Quote it with a link back. Do not pass it off as your own.
  • Two free checks a week. Applying does not guarantee a slot. You hear back either way.

Changes

If this page changes in a way that matters, the effective date at the top moves and the change is described here in one line. Small wording fixes do not get an entry.

The whole thing in one breath: I collect what you type, use it to do the thing you asked for, keep it only as long as that takes, and delete it when you say so.
