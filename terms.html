The Measured-or-Free guarantee

Every paid fix ships with tracking, and the guarantee works like this:

  • One number, agreed up front. For example quote requests a week, and the system that records it.
  • A baseline first. I record that number before I change anything.
  • Measured at 30 days. Thirty days after delivery I read the number again from the same system.
  • If it cannot be shown, you get every fee back. A full refund of what you paid me, within 15 days. Outside costs passed through at cost, such as a phone line, are not refundable.
  • What it is not. It is not a promise that the number goes up by any amount, and it is not a promise of rankings, leads, calls, or revenue. Nobody can honestly promise those.
  • When it does not apply. If the tracking is removed or changed, the pages or systems it measures are changed without telling me, or my access is removed before the 30 days are up.
